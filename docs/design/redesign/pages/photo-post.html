Walking the south coast of Crete

2025-06-10by Example Author1 min read

The south coast of Crete has no road along most of it, so the paths are old and the villages are reached by boat or on foot.

The route

  1. Start at a harbour with a morning ferry.
  2. Walk west along the cliffs; the path is marked with red dots.
  3. Sleep in the next village, wherever there is a room.
  4. Repeat until your feet say stop.

Carry twice the water you think you need. The springs on the map are seasonal.

Light

The best light comes late: the sun sets over the sea, and the cliffs turn orange for twenty minutes.
