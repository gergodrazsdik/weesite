I went to the Deutsches Museum in Munich on a grey, wet February day, the kind of day a big indoor museum is made for. Before I even got inside, I stopped to photograph the bicycle parking.

![Rows of parked bicycles in a covered arcade with heavy stone pillars, wall lamps and a concrete ceiling, with bare trees outside](/media/9e1ab7afa008-bicycle-parking.jpg "The arcade under the building, out of the rain")

## A good place for bicycles

The bicycles stand in a covered arcade under one of the museum buildings. Heavy square pillars hold up a concrete ceiling, there are old lamps on the walls, and the floor was still wet from people wheeling their bikes in.

It is a small thing, but it pleased me. Someone had thought about where people would leave their bicycles and given them a dry, solid space instead of a rack in the rain. And it was full, on a cold Saturday.

## Notes from inside

- The museum is huge. Pick a few sections and accept that you will come back.
- Arrive early; the popular halls get crowded by late morning.
- The building itself is worth looking at, inside and out. So, apparently, is its bicycle parking.

I spent most of the day with old machines and instruments. But this picture of a quiet arcade full of bicycles is the one I keep coming back to.
