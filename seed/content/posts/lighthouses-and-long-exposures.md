Long exposures smooth water and clouds into soft shapes, which suits subjects that don't move, such as lighthouses and bridges.

![A small white lighthouse on a rocky point, seen from above, in dark blue water](/media/82adc6cea959-lighthouse.jpg)

## Settings that worked

| Setting | Value |
| --- | --- |
| Aperture | f/8 to f/11 |
| ISO | 100 |
| Exposure | 10 to 30 s |
| Filter | 6-stop neutral density |

## Bridges in daylight

On a bright day a long exposure turns the water flat and calm, and the bridge's lines stand out against the sky.

![A long cable-stayed bridge across a lake under a blue sky](/media/c413646f0110-lake-bridge.jpg)
