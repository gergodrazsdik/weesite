On my last day on Euboea I had a few hours before going back to Athens, so I stopped at Agios Minas, a small bay near Chalkida. I had picked it from the map because it looked quiet. It was.

![A white sailing boat at anchor in a calm bay, with a low hill and a range of mountains behind under big white clouds](/media/c6dc619e172d-quiet-bay.jpg "Agios Minas bay, with one boat at anchor")

## The bay

A curve of shore, clear water, a handful of boats at anchor, and across the bay a low rocky hill with a long line of mountains behind it. Big white clouds were piling up over the mountains, the kind that look solid enough to stand on.

I sat on a rock for an hour and did very little. Someone rowed out to a sailing boat, spent a while on deck, then rowed back. That was the main event.

## End-of-trip habits

The last day of a trip is easy to waste on packing and worrying about the journey home. I've started keeping a few hours free for a place like this, on the way to the airport or the port, with nothing to see. It gives the trip an ending instead of a scramble.

What I do with those hours:

- write down the things I'll forget first: names, small moments, the best meal;
- take one or two last photos, without trying hard;
- sit still for a while.

Then I went back to the car, and the trip was over properly.
