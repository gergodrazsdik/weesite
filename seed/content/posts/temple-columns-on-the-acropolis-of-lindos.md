Lindos is a village of white houses on the east coast of Rhodes, with an old acropolis on the rock above it. I went in May, when the days are warm but not yet heavy.

![A row of tall stone columns of an ancient temple on a hilltop, with the sea and a blue sky behind and a few visitors resting nearby](/media/3edf2dc3489c-lindos-acropolis.jpg "The columns of the temple of Athena, on top of the rock")

## The climb

The path goes up from the village through narrow lanes and then along the rock. It is not long, but it is steep and there is little shade, so I started early. The steps are worn smooth; good shoes help more than you would think.

At the top the ground is open and pale, and the columns of the temple of Athena stand in a line at the edge, with nothing behind them but sea and sky. People sit on the low walls and look out. So did I, for longer than I planned.

## A few notes

- Take water; there is not much once you leave the village.
- Wind comes and goes up there, even on calm days.
- The sky at midday is a deep, flat blue. It suits the stone, but watch the exposure on the columns.

On the way down, the village looks completely different: a pattern of white roofs and courtyards that you cannot see from inside it.
