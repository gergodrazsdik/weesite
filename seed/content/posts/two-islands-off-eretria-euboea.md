After [Karystos](/blog/old-anchor-on-the-seafront-at-karystos) I moved north along Euboea to Eretria, a small town on the channel between the island and the mainland. Out in the water, not far from the shore, sit two little islands: a longer one covered in trees, and a tiny one next to it.

![A small, low island covered in trees and a tiny rocky islet in a calm blue sea, with hazy mountains behind](/media/940a5a352d75-two-islands.jpg "Agia Triada and Aspronisos from the shore")

## Looking rather than visiting

I learned their names from a map: Agia Triada and Aspronisos. I never went to either. I was happy to look at them from the shore every evening, which is what I did.

The water was calm most days, and in the afternoon the mountains behind faded into a blue haze.

## A note on haze

I usually wait for clear days to photograph landscapes, but here the haze helps. It separates the layers: dark green island in front, blue water, pale mountains behind. On a sharp, clear morning the same view looked flat, because everything was equally bright.

My settings were nothing special. I used a longer focal length to bring the islands closer and stack the layers on top of each other, and I exposed a little darker than the camera wanted, so the water kept its colour.

If you stay in Eretria, find a bench on the seafront in the late afternoon. The islands don't do anything. That is what makes them good to look at.
