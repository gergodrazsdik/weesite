I went back to Quebec City in summer, partly to see whether it was the same place I had walked through in the snow. It isn't. The outdoor centre at Sainte-Foy, around the Lacs Laberge, was all green shade and birdsong.

![A dirt path curving through a dense green forest of tall, thin trees, with sunlight coming through the leaves](/media/06bd645d47dc-lake-trail.jpg "Mid-morning on the path between the lakes")

## The walk

There is no grand route here. The paths loop around the lakes and through the woods, and you can make the walk as long or as short as you like. I did two slow loops and stopped often.

- The paths are packed earth, easy in dry weather and soft after rain.
- The trees are tall and close together, so it stays cool even at noon.
- Mosquitoes find you the moment you stand still. Bring repellent.

## Photographing a forest

Forests are hard to photograph. Everything is the same green, and the eye has nowhere to rest. What helped was the path itself: a curve that leads into the picture gives the trees something to stand around. I waited for the sun to come through behind the trunks, kept the exposure a little dark so the highlights held, and left it at that.

If you only know the city in winter, as I did after [a week of snow](/blog/a-winter-week-in-quebec-city), it is worth coming back when the leaves are out.
