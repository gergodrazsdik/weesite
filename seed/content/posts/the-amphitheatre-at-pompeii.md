Most visitors to Pompeii stay near the main entrance and the busiest streets. I kept walking, and at the far end of the site I came to the amphitheatre, quiet and wide open under the April sky.

![The stone seating tiers of an ancient amphitheatre, with grass on the upper rows and umbrella pines beyond the rim](/media/fe7ef2decb3f-amphitheatre.jpg "The seating tiers on a spring afternoon")

## Standing in the arena

Walking out onto the flat sand floor, I had the place almost to myself. The stone tiers rise all around, the upper rows grown over with grass, and beyond the rim a line of umbrella pines stands against the sky. It's very still, which makes it hard to picture it full of people shouting.

I sat on one of the lower tiers for a while and ate an orange. A couple of other people wandered in, looked around and left again.

## Getting there

- It's a long walk from the main entrance, over uneven paving. Wear proper shoes.
- There's very little shade on the way. Take a hat and water.
- Leave it for the afternoon, when the rest of the site is at its most crowded.

The famous houses and frescoes are worth the crowds. But if I go back, I'll head straight for this end first.
