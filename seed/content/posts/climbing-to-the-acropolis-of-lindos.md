The acropolis of Lindos sits on a rock high above the village and the sea, on the east coast of Rhodes. You see it from far off, and the walk up through the village is part of the visit.

![Tall weathered stone columns on a rocky hilltop, with the blue sea behind them](/media/761ec44946e4-lindos-hill.jpg "The columns at the top, with the sea behind")

## The way up

The village is a tangle of narrow white lanes, and the path climbs through them, then up stone steps to the top. It isn't long, but in late September it was still hot by mid-morning and there's little shade on the last part. I stopped often and pretended it was for the view. It partly was.

## At the top

The columns stand against the sky with nothing behind them but sea. Up close the stone is worn and pitted, and pale gold rather than white. The wind was strong, which was a relief after the climb.

- Go first thing in the morning, before the heat and the crowds.
- Wear shoes with grip; the steps are polished smooth.
- Bring water, and drink it before you feel you need it.

On the way down I sat in a café in the village and looked back up at the rock. From below, the columns are tiny. It's hard to believe you were just standing among them.
