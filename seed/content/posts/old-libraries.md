Old monastery libraries are some of the best rooms to photograph: painted ceilings, long rows of shelves, and light from tall windows. From outside, the library is often just another wing of the monastery.

![A monastery wing with a red tiled roof above an old town, under a cloudy sky](/media/690a2c4f2bca-monastery-library.jpg)

## Without a flash

Flash is almost never allowed, and it flattens the room anyway. Instead:

- brace the camera on a pillar or a railing;
- shoot several frames and keep the sharpest;
- expose for the windows and lift the shadows later.

## Respect the rules

Many libraries allow photos only at certain times, or for a small fee. Ask first.
