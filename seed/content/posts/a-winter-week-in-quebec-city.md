In December the sun sets before five, so most of the pictures from this week are night pictures.

![A lit-up tower above the city lights at dusk](/media/363ad5a444bf-city-night.jpg)

## Night streets

Snow reflects the street lights, so night scenes are brighter than you expect. Bracket the exposures.

![A bicycle half buried in snow, locked to a post at night](/media/ecbf632d0a3e-snowy-bike.jpg)

## Keeping the camera warm

Batteries drain fast in the cold. Keep a spare in an inside pocket and swap them often.
