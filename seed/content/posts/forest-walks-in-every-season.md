Walking one route through the year teaches you more about a place than one long trip.

## Summer

![Tall spruce trunks in a dense forest with moss on the ground](/media/c0d16c7a20e9-spruce-forest.jpg)

The spruce forest is dark and cool even at noon.

## Winter

![A forest road covered in snow between snow-laden trees](/media/5f3313d573ee-snowy-road.jpg)

Snow makes the road the brightest thing in the frame; expose for it.

## Autumn, at the edge of the forest

![A single red and yellow marigold flower among green leaves](/media/e2f2cf3360b3-marigolds.jpg)

The gardens at the edge of the village keep their colour until the first frost.
