It snowed all day, and by evening the streets of Quebec City had gone quiet in the way only heavy snow can manage. I went out late for a walk along 25e Rue, mostly to see what it looked like.

![A car almost buried in deep snow on a residential street at night, lit by a single street lamp](/media/a965c519c36d-quiet-street.jpg "A parked car under the snow, close to midnight")

## The street under snow

Every parked car had become a white mound. The one in the picture still showed a bit of its grille and one wing mirror, as if it were peering out. The street lamps turned the snow orange, and the sky, full of light bouncing back from the ground, never got properly dark.

The only sounds were my boots and, far off, a snowplough working its way round the neighbourhood. Nobody else was out.

## Walking in it

- Walk in the tyre tracks if there are any; fresh snow above the ankle is tiring.
- Keep the camera inside your coat between shots, so the battery lasts.
- Expose a little brighter than the camera wants, or the snow comes out grey.

By the next morning the ploughs had been through, the cars were being dug out and the street was ordinary again. I'm glad I went out when I did.
