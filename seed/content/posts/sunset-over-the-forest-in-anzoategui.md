I was staying near Chaguaramal, in the state of Anzoátegui in eastern Venezuela, and most evenings looked the same: a hazy sky that faded to grey without much fuss. Then one evening the clouds broke up just as the sun went down.

![Pink and orange clouds in an evening sky above the dark outline of a forest](/media/46ff964dab23-forest-sunset.jpg "The sky over the forest, a few minutes after sunset")

## Ten minutes

The whole thing lasted maybe ten minutes. The undersides of the clouds lit up pink, then orange, and the gaps between them turned a clean pale blue. The trees below went completely black, which I liked: the forest became a single line along the bottom of the frame, and the sky got all the attention.

I didn't have a tripod, so I leaned against a fence post and took a lot of frames. The ones that worked had the treeline low in the picture, with plenty of sky above it.

## What I'd tell myself

- Don't pack up when the sun goes down. The colour often comes after.
- Let the ground go dark rather than trying to brighten it.
- Watch the sky behind you too. It was pink there as well.

By the time I walked back the frogs had started up and it was properly dark. I've seen bigger sunsets, but few that changed so quickly.
