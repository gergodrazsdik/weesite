Karystos is a small town at the southern end of Euboea, with a long seafront and a harbour that is busy in the morning and sleepy by noon. I stayed a few days in September, when the sea is still warm and the crowds have gone home.

![A large dark old anchor set on a lawn beside the sea, with trees and a mountain on the far shore](/media/82b57ce7e560-old-anchor.jpg "The anchor and the bay on a clear September morning")

## The anchor

On a patch of lawn by the water stands an old anchor, much taller than me, dark and set on a low base. I don't know where it came from or which ship it held, and I couldn't find a sign. It just stands there facing the bay, and people walk past it with their dogs and their ice creams.

I walked past it every day, and on the third morning I finally took a picture, with the sea and the far shore behind it.

## A slow seaside routine

My days there had a simple shape:

1. Coffee on the seafront, early.
2. A swim before the sun got high.
3. A long lunch, then a book in the shade.
4. A walk along the water in the evening, past the anchor again.

Nothing in that list is remarkable, and that was the point of the trip. Some places you visit for the sights. Karystos I visited to do very little, slowly, and it was good at that.
