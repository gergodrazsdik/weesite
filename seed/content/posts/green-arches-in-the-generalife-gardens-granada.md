The Generalife is the garden side of the Alhambra, on the slope above the palaces. I went on a bright April morning, half expecting fountains and nothing else. What I remember most is a path.

![A straight gravel path running through a tunnel of green climbing plants trained over arches](/media/9914c25a852a-cypress-alley.jpg "One of the covered walks in the Generalife")

## The tunnel

It is a long, straight walk of fine gravel, covered by arches with climbing plants trained over them, so the whole path becomes a green tunnel. At the far end the light is bright and white. Inside it is cool and quiet, and the sound of water comes from somewhere off to the side.

I walked it three times. The first time I was in a hurry to see the rest. The second time I slowed down. The third time I stopped halfway and just stood in the shade.

## What I took home

I don't have room for anything like this in my small garden, but I took one idea home: a garden doesn't have to be seen all at once. A covered path hides the view and then gives it back at the end, and that makes the arrival feel like something.

At home I've started training a climbing rose over a simple metal arch by the gate. It is one arch, not a tunnel, and it will take years. But on the way to the door you now pass under something green, and I like that more than I expected.
