After a morning in the narrow streets of Córdoba, the gardens of the Alcázar de los Reyes Cristianos felt like a deep breath. I went in late April, when the roses were starting and the air was warm but not yet hot.

![A formal garden with flower beds in front, a tall palm tree and a row of dark cypresses behind](/media/f59a58e2ca54-palace-gardens.jpg "Palms, cypresses and roses in late April")

## Layers of green

What I remember most is the layering. Low flower beds in front, then clipped hedges, then a row of tall, dark cypresses, and above it all the spreading crowns of palm trees. Every few steps the view lined up differently, and a new path opened between the hedges.

There's water too, in long pools and fountains, and the sound of it follows you around. Even with other visitors, the place felt calm.

## Tips for a visit

- Go in the morning, before the midday heat in the open parts.
- Walk the edges as well as the central paths; some of the quietest corners are there.
- Leave time to sit in the shade and just listen to the water.

I spent longer in the gardens than inside the building, and I don't regret it. It's one of the places I think of when someone asks me what a good garden looks like.
