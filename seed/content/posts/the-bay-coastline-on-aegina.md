On my last morning on Aegina I skipped the harbour café and walked along the coast instead. September is a good month for it: the sea is still warm, and the sun no longer pushes you indoors by eleven.

![A curving bay of clear blue and turquoise water, with a sandy path and low green shrubs along the shore and a wooded headland beyond](/media/7b6dfd0d325a-island-coast.jpg "The bay from the path above the beach")

## Along the shore

The path runs along low sandy cliffs above the water. Below, the beach is a mix of pebbles and sand, and the sea goes from pale turquoise at the edge to deep blue further out. Shrubs grow right to the edge of the slope, and a few pines lean out over it.

I passed a handful of people swimming and one man fishing from the rocks. Otherwise it was quiet, apart from the sea and the cicadas.

## What to bring

- Water and a hat; there is very little shade on the open stretches.
- Shoes you can walk in on loose sand.
- Something to swim in. You will want to.

After a few days of [the harbour](/blog/the-harbour-chapel-of-agios-nikolaos-on-aegina) and its boats, this was a good way to end: no plan, no destination, just following the line of the bay until it was time to turn back for the ferry.
