Espresso is a feedback loop: change one thing, taste, and change it again.

![A cup of espresso with crema, seen from above on a white table](/media/4c01a5e824a1-espresso.jpg)

## A starting recipe

| Dose | Yield | Time | Temperature |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 18 g | 36 g | 25 to 30 s | 93 °C |

## What to change

- **Sour and thin:** grind finer.
- **Bitter and harsh:** grind coarser.
- **Fine but flat:** try a longer ratio, such as 1:2.5.

Keep notes. A plain text file is enough, and it [lasts longer than any app](/blog/small-things-the-audio-plug).
