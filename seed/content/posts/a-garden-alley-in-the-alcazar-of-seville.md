The palace rooms of the Alcázar in Seville are what most people come for. I went through them, then spent longer in the gardens than inside, which I think is the right proportion.

![A long, straight paved path between neatly trimmed green hedges, with tall palm trees and dense greenery on both sides](/media/8acdc7a1f38d-garden-alley.jpg "One of the long alleys in the outer gardens")

## Hedges and palms

The gardens are a series of spaces, each a little different. Near the palace they are formal, with tiled paths, fountains and low walls. Further out, they open up into long alleys like this one, with hedges trimmed into neat boxes and palms rising high above them.

In April, the hedges were fresh green, the orange trees were in flower, and the air smelled of them everywhere. The shade from the palms made the paths pleasant even at midday.

## Things I noticed

- The geometry is strict but the planting is generous. It never feels stiff.
- There are benches in quiet corners. Use them.
- The water is everywhere: channels, basins, small fountains. You hear it before you see it.

I have a small garden at home, and I came back wanting more straight lines and more water. I have since managed one of the two. The hedge is still at the stage where you have to imagine it.
