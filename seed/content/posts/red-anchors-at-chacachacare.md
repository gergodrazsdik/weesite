Chacachacare is a small island off the coast of Trinidad, and the only way there is by boat. We landed on a bright spring morning, and the first thing I saw on the quay was a heap of anchors painted a red you could see from the water.

![Several anchors painted bright red lying on a concrete quay, with moored boats behind them](/media/19e6c611344d-anchors.jpg "The anchors on the quay, on a bright spring morning")

## Why I stopped

I'm not sure whether they were put there as a display or just left where they were last needed. Either way, someone had cared enough to paint them, and against the grey concrete they looked almost like a sculpture. The flukes stuck up at odd angles and threw long shadows across the ground.

I walked around them a few times looking for the right angle. Low down, from the side, the anchors fill the frame and the boats behind become a soft backdrop. From higher up they just look like scrap.
