I'd been told that the best autumn colours are a long drive from Quebec City. Then, on an ordinary walk near Avenue du Bourg-Royal, at the northern edge of the city, I came out of the trees and saw this.

![A hillside covered in red, orange and yellow trees above a dry meadow with a path running through it](/media/c7990f7a267b-autumn-forest.jpg "The hillside in mid-October")

## The colours

The whole slope was mixed forest, and each kind of tree had turned at its own pace. The maples were the loudest, deep red and orange, with yellow birches between them and a few dark evergreens holding the green. In the meadow below, the grass had gone the colour of straw.

It changes fast. A week earlier it would have been mostly green, and a week later, after one windy night, a lot of those leaves would be on the ground.

## Notes for next year

- Watch the trees close to home from late September. Peak colour doesn't wait for a weekend.
- Overcast days are fine. Soft light keeps the reds from looking garish.
- Look for a place with a view across a slope, not just into the trees.

I walked to the top of the path and back in about an hour. It's the closest I've come to a perfect autumn walk, and it was almost on my doorstep.
