For a while my mornings went the same way: open the laptop, check messages, open three tabs, start two things, finish neither. What fixed it, mostly, was a cup of coffee.

![A cup of espresso with crema, seen from above on a white table](/media/4c01a5e824a1-espresso.jpg "The first cup of the day")

## The habit

It's simple enough to feel silly writing down:

1. Make one espresso. Not in a hurry; the few minutes at the machine are part of it.
2. While it brews, choose one task. Write it on a sticky note.
3. Sit down and work on that task, and only that, until the cup is empty and cold.

An espresso goes cold in about the time I can hold real focus, so it works as a timer that doesn't beep. When the cup is cold I look up, check whatever needs checking, and decide whether to make another.

## Why it works for me

The coffee isn't the point. The ritual is. Making it gives me a moment to decide what matters before the day decides for me, and the sticky note keeps me honest when I'm tempted to open something else.

- It doesn't need an app or a setting.
- It ends on its own, so I don't feel trapped.
- It's limited by caffeine, which keeps me from doing it six times a day.

Some days I still get pulled off course. But the first hour is nearly always good now, and that's enough.
