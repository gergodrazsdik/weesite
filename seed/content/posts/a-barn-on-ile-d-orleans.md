Île d'Orléans is an island in the St. Lawrence River, a short drive from Quebec City across a bridge. It's mostly farms, orchards and small villages, and I spent a summer day cycling along its main road.

![A pale grey wooden barn with a gambrel roof on a mown green lawn, with tall trees behind](/media/82daa660a206-island-barn.jpg "The barn I stopped for, early in the afternoon")

## The barn

There are a lot of barns on the island, but this one made me get off the bike. It's small and neat, painted a soft grey with white trim, and the roof has that double slope that gives barns their shape. The lawn in front had just been mown, and the trees behind were at their fullest green.

I liked how ordinary it was. It wasn't a monument, just a building someone kept in good shape, which seemed to be true of most of the island.

## A day on the island

- A bike is the nicest way round, but expect hills and some traffic on the main road.
- Farm stands sell fruit and vegetables in season; bring cash and a bag.
- Leave time for detours down the side roads towards the river.

I came back sunburnt, with a bag of strawberries and too many photos of barns. This is the one I kept.
