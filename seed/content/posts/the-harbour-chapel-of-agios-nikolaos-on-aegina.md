The ferry from Piraeus takes you into Aegina's harbour, and as it turns in, a small white chapel stands at the end of the quay. It was the first building I saw on the island, and I walked back to it every day.

![A small whitewashed chapel with two rounded domes topped by crosses, on a paved quay by the sea under a clear blue sky](/media/8f843f047ec2-port-chapel.jpg "The chapel of Agios Nikolaos at the harbour entrance")

## A white shape against blue

The chapel is tiny. Two rounded domes, thick whitewashed walls, a couple of small windows, a cross on each dome. Agios Nikolaos is the patron saint of sailors, so a chapel to him at the harbour mouth makes sense.

What makes it beautiful is how simple it is. In the morning, against a clear sky, it is just white on blue, with the sea behind. There is a bench next to it where people sit and watch the boats come in.

## Photographing white walls

- Whitewash in full sun is very bright. I lowered the exposure until the walls kept their texture.
- Early morning and late afternoon give the domes some shading, which shows their shape.
- Lamp posts and wires get in the way from most angles. Walk around until they line up behind the building.
