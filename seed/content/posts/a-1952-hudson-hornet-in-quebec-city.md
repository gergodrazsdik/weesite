The St. Patrick's Day parade in Quebec City brought out a cold, bright day and a crowd dressed in every shade of green. Among the bands and the floats came a black 1952 Hudson Hornet, polished so well it reflected the people watching it.

![A black 1950s car with chrome trim driving down a street past a crowd dressed in green](/media/bfaa4bf486ae-classic-car.jpg "The Hudson Hornet rolling past the crowd")

## The car

I'm not a car person, but I can see why people love these. It's long, low and rounded, with a heavy chrome grille and bumpers that look like they could stop a bus. The whitewall tyres finish it off. It moved at walking pace, and people along the barrier pointed and waved.

## Photographing a parade

- Pick a spot early, ideally at a corner, so things come towards you.
- Use a fast shutter speed; parade cars move faster than you'd think.
- Keep an eye on the background. Here the crowd in green tells you which parade it is.
