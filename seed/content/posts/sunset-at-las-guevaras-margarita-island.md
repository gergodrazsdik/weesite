Most evenings on Margarita I watched the sun go down over the water like everyone else. One evening near Las Guevaras I walked the other way, away from the beach, into dry scrub with a few trees and some tall cactus.

![A lone tree in silhouette against an orange sunset sky with scattered clouds, over dry grassland](/media/27fe780c1c61-island-sunset.jpg "Shooting straight into the sun, for once on purpose")

## Into the light

There was one tree standing apart from the others, and the sun was dropping right behind it. I usually avoid pointing the camera at the sun, because the sky turns white and everything else goes black. This time I wanted exactly that: the tree as a dark shape, and the clouds lit from below.

A few things helped:

- I exposed for the sky, not the ground, and let the shadows fall away.
- I moved a step to the left until the sun sat just at the edge of the branches.
- I took a lot of frames, because the light changed every minute.

## Afterwards

Back at the room I brought up the shadows a little, enough to show the grass but not so much that the picture lost its mood. The result is not a postcard. It looks like what it was: a warm, still evening, and a few minutes of standing very quietly in a field.
